Optional — shown next to the result so you can sanity-check the count, doesn't change detection.
Works like a bubble-sheet scanner: finds the grid the boxes sit on, then measures the ink inside every grid position — even boxes whose outline an X has damaged.

Tap a box on the photo to flip it between marked and blank. Tap empty space to add a mark the scan missed.

If the count looks wrong, tap this and paste the result into chat — it's a text summary of what the detector found (sizes, darkness scores, where on the page), no image data.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
